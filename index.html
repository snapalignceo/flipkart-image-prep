Image Prep for Flipkart

Drop your product photos here. Each one is auto-detected, centered, and scaled so the product lands at the same size and position on a pure-white 2000×2000 canvas across your whole batch, then exported as a Flipkart-spec JPEG — ready to save straight to your listing folder. An in-browser AI model also suggests a category and color for each photo, so you can tag a batch of listings faster. Runs entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded anywhere.

About fit whole photo: auto-align above crops in on just the detected product and zooms it to fill ~85% of the frame. This option skips that entirely — it takes the photo exactly as uploaded and scales the whole thing down (or up) to fit inside the 2000×2000 square, padding whichever side is shorter with white. Nothing is cropped, redrawn, or altered, only resized as a whole and centered. Turn this on when you want the full original framing kept (e.g. the photo already has a clean white background and the right crop) rather than having the tool try to detect and isolate the product.
About true white background: it replaces only pixels that closely match the backdrop color sampled from this photo's own corners — never a guess about shape or position. If a background pixel isn't a close match (e.g. a strong shadow or vignette), it's left as-is rather than forced white, so the worst case is a background that's only partly whitened. The one real limitation: a product color very close to the backdrop (an off-white or cream product on a light-grey backdrop, say) may also get lightened, since color alone can't tell those apart — check the result and turn this off for that specific batch if it happens.
Note: this pads and resizes — it doesn't cut out a busy background. If your photo wasn't shot on a plain, even-colored background, auto-align may miscrop it. For best results, and for a true background swap, photograph on a plain white or light backdrop before uploading here — or turn on AI background removal above, which doesn't have this limitation.
About AI background removal: this runs a real image-segmentation model (U²-Net) in your browser to find the product's actual outline, rather than guessing from color — so it works on busy, textured, or uneven backgrounds the true-white toggle above can't fix. When it's on, it replaces both auto-align detection and true-white-background for that photo. It downloads its model (~4.5 MB, Apache-2.0 licensed, free to use) once per browser session from a public CDN and takes roughly a second per photo; your photos still never leave the browser. If the model can't load (e.g. a blocked network), processing falls back to the toggle above automatically.
About AI tagging: category and color are suggestions from a general-purpose on-device image model (MobileNet) plus a color-averaging step — not a model trained on your specific catalog. They're a starting point to speed up bulk tagging, not a final answer: always review the dropdown on each card before you use it in a listing. The model itself downloads once from a public CDN; your photos still never leave the browser.
